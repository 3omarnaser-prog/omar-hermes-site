Omar Hermes is an individual-operated integration intended to help Omar work with his own TikTok account through Hermes Agent and Composio.

This website explains the integration and its proposed data practices. It does not provide a public login, accept account credentials, or connect your TikTok account by itself.

TikTok OAuth → Composio connection → Hermes Agent → operator-requested tasks

Intended use

Retrieve authorised profile information, account statistics and video metadata for the operator's own content workflow. Actual availability depends on granted scopes and TikTok's app status.

Permission before action

Publishing or changing content is not enabled by this website. Such actions require the operator's explicit instruction, the relevant TikTok permission and any required platform approval.

Current status

A TikTok developer app and a test sandbox have been created. TikTok authorisation and an end-to-end account connection have not yet been verified. This is not a public service or an approved public-posting application.

Operator and contact

The individual operator is Omar. For questions, concerns or deletion requests, contact 3omarnaser@gmail.com. There is no public registration or customer support portal at this stage.

عن التكامل
