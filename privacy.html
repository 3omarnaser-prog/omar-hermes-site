Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · Operator: Omar (individual)

This policy describes the intended personal TikTok integration named Omar Hermes. It covers the informational website and the workflow using Hermes Agent with Composio. The integration is under configuration and is not open for public registration.

1. Purpose and scope

The intended user is the operator accessing his own authorised TikTok account. Data is used to perform tasks the operator requests, such as reviewing account information, statistics and video metadata. Connecting an account does not by itself authorise public posting or every possible tool action.

2. Information involved

  • Account and content data: information TikTok supplies within the permissions actually granted, which may include account identifiers, profile details, account statistics, video identifiers and video metadata.
  • Connection information: OAuth authorisation records and access or refresh tokens handled by TikTok, Composio and the configured integration components. TikTok passwords should be entered only on TikTok's own authentication pages.
  • Task and diagnostic records: instructions, tool arguments, API responses, execution status and conversation or diagnostic logs may be processed or retained by the configured services and the local Hermes environment.
  • Website requests: this static website contains no analytics code, tracking scripts, forms or application cookies. The hosting provider may process technical request information under its own policies.

3. Processing and third-party services

The intended data path is TikTok → Composio → Hermes Agent. When needed to carry out a requested AI task, relevant instructions and tool results may also be sent to the AI provider configured in Hermes. The operator must review and approve the relevant account permissions and provider choices before use. Secrets are not intentionally included in prompts or public website files.

These providers apply their own terms, security controls, privacy policies and retention practices. This policy does not promise zero retention, local-only processing or deletion from a provider's systems merely because an account is disconnected.

4. Storage and retention

Connection data and execution logs may be stored by Composio; conversation history, tool results and diagnostic logs may be stored in the operator's Hermes environment. The applicable AI provider may process or retain request data according to its service terms. No verified integration-wide automatic deletion schedule has been established. Data should be limited to what is necessary for the operator's requested tasks, and local records should be reviewed and removed when no longer needed, subject to any applicable obligations.

For Composio's current retention details, consult Composio data retention documentation. No provider-specific retention period is promised by this page.

5. Control, disconnection and deletion

The operator can stop requesting tasks, revoke the application's access in TikTok and remove the connected account in Composio. Revocation prevents future authorised access but does not automatically erase records already retained elsewhere. Local history and downloaded results must be reviewed separately; deletion of provider-held records must be requested or performed through that provider's available controls. This integration has no separate public self-service deletion portal.

Contact for questions or deletion requests: email 3omarnaser@gmail.com. The operator handles requests manually; there is no automated deletion service. Do not send passwords, tokens or private account data in public channels.

6. Security and access boundaries

This static website does not store account credentials or run TikTok API calls. The operator is responsible for protecting the Hermes environment, connected accounts and developer credentials, keeping secrets out of public repositories, and limiting tool permissions. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes and third-party affiliation

If account access, providers, data uses or retention settings change materially, the published policy must be updated before that change is relied upon. Omar Hermes is an independent personal integration, not TikTok, Composio, Nous Research or an official product of those organisations.
